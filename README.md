# Smart-Logistics-Control-Center

## Overview

Smart Logistics Control Center is a JavaFX Decision Support System designed to simulate real-world supply chain operations.

The application helps Operations Managers monitor shipments, detect disruptions, evaluate transportation alternatives, and make data-driven logistics decisions in real time.

Instead of focusing on a single transportation method, the system manages multimodal logistics including sea, air, rail and road transportation.

The goal is to minimize delays, transportation costs and operational risks while maintaining customer satisfaction.

## The Business Problem

Every day, thousands of products travel across the world using multiple transportation methods such as ships, airplanes, trains and trucks.

During transportation, unexpected events frequently occur:

- Port congestion
- Severe weather conditions
- Customs inspections
- Railway strikes
- Road accidents
- Delayed vessels
- Warehouse capacity issues
- Urgent customer requests

These disruptions create difficult operational decisions.

Should the shipment continue on its current route?

Should it be rerouted using another transport method?

Is the additional transportation cost justified by avoiding production delays or customer penalties?

In real companies these decisions are often taken under time pressure while considering costs, delivery deadlines, operational priorities and customer satisfaction.

Smart Logistics Control Center simulates these real-world scenarios by providing an interactive decision-support system that helps evaluate logistics alternatives and recommend the most effective solution.
